## **1. Introduction**

  The TSEC/KW-26 cryptographic equipment was the first wide-scale, electronic encryption system used by the United States and its military intelligence. It was developed with the National Security Agency (NSA), with the purpose of ensuring the security of transit communications (COMSEC). The TSEC/KW-26 aimed to shield COMSEC operations from exploitation and disruption, focusing on maintaining the integrity and availability of its systems (Klein 1). Through the cryptographic equipment's lifetime, it achieved the transition of encryption from paper-based to electronic hardware, and set standards that would help drive forward automatic software in cybersecurity.
